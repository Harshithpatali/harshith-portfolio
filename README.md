## Analytical approach

1. Define the business or research question and unit of analysis.
2. Inspect data quality, source grain, missingness and temporal structure.
3. Establish simple, appropriate statistical or predictive baselines.
4. Apply statistical inference or modelling with leakage-aware validation.
5. Evaluate uncertainty, failure modes and practical decision implications.
6. Report assumptions and limitations alongside headline metrics.
