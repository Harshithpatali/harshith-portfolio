03 / How I work

Modeling is not the finish line.

My preferred workflow is designed to make results defensible before making them impressive.

01

Frame the decision

Define the business question, outcome, intervention and decision metric before choosing a model.

02

Validate the data

Check grain, leakage, missingness, balance and point-in-time availability.

03

Build baselines

Establish simple benchmarks before reaching for complex models.

04

Model & quantify

Use appropriate validation, uncertainty estimates and causal assumptions where needed.

05

Explain the result

Use confidence intervals, SHAP, treatment effects, calibration or other evidence suited to the question.

06

Ship the decision

Expose the result through an API, dashboard or reproducible workflow that someone can actually use.

“I don't treat model accuracy as the final answer. The goal is a defensible decision system.”
